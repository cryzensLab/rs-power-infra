What we manufacture

Control panels we build

LT distribution panels

Low-tension panels that distribute power safely across your plant or building.

PCC panels

Power Control Centres that manage and protect the main incoming supply.

MCC panels

Motor Control Centres to start, stop and protect motors from one place.

APFC panels

Automatic Power Factor Correction to improve power factor and help avoid utility penalties.

Electrical automation panels

Panels that automate machines, pumps and processes.
