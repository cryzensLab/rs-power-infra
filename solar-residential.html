Solar for Homes

Rooftop solar for homes and housing societies, with help claiming the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

Solar rooftop for residences

Lower bills from your own roof

A rooftop solar system powers your home with free sunlight, and the government helps pay for it.

Much lower bills

Your panels cover most of your daytime use; extra power goes to the grid through net-metering.

Government subsidy

Up to ₹78,000 off your system under PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana.*

Paperwork handled

We register you on the national portal and handle DISCOM approval for you.

PM Surya Ghar subsidy

How much you can get

  • 1 kW system₹30,000
  • 2 kW system₹60,000
  • 3 kW and above₹78,000

*Central subsidy for homes as per MNRE guidelines. Housing societies get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities. Amounts and eligibility may change; check pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Free home survey

    We check your roof and your electricity bill.

  2. Design & registration

    We size the system and register it for the subsidy.

  3. Installation & net-meter

    Genuine Waaree panels installed in a few days.

  4. Subsidy credited

    After inspection, the subsidy reaches your bank account.
