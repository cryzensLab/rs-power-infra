Government of India scheme

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Get a central government subsidy of up to ₹78,000 on rooftop solar for your home and enjoy up to 300 units of free electricity every month. We guide you through registration, paperwork, installation and net-metering.

  • ₹78,000Max. subsidy for homes
  • 300Units free / month*
  • ~7%Collateral-free loans*
Check My Eligibility Official portal
Your subsidy for a home system of
₹78,000
  • Up to 2 kW₹30,000 / kW
  • Additional capacity up to 3 kW₹18,000 / kW
  • Housing societies (common facilities)₹18,000 / kW
  1. 01
    Register

    Sign up on the national portal with your electricity connection details.

  2. 02
    Apply & approval

    Submit the rooftop solar application and get DISCOM feasibility approval.

  3. 03
    Install & net-meter

    We install your Waaree system and coordinate net-meter installation.

  4. 04
    Get subsidy

    After inspection, the subsidy is credited directly to your bank account.

*Subsidy amounts, free units and loan terms are as per MNRE scheme guidelines and may change. Eligibility subject to scheme rules and DISCOM approval.
