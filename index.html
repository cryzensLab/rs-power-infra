Why industries go solar

What solar does for your business

Lower power costs

Factories use most of their power in the daytime, exactly when solar produces it.

Fast payback

Most industrial systems pay for themselves in about 3–5 years, then keep saving for 25+.

Tax savings

Businesses that own their system can claim 40% accelerated depreciation in the first year.*

  • Manufacturing
  • Warehouses
  • Textiles
  • Food processing
  • Offices & malls
  • Hospitals & schools

*Under Section 32 of the Income Tax Act, for profitable businesses that own the system. Please confirm with your tax advisor.
