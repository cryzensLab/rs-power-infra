Our story

Young in years, strong in purpose

RS Power and Infra started with a simple belief: switching to solar should be easy, transparent and affordable. In just one year we have grown rapidly, helping families, shops and businesses cut their electricity bills with reliable solar systems.

As an authorised Waaree franchise, we bring you genuine products from one of India's largest solar manufacturers, backed by our own local team for site survey, design, subsidy paperwork, installation and after-sales support.

How we work

From first call to first unit generated

1

Free site survey

We visit your property, check roof space, shading and your electricity usage.

2

System design & quote

A custom design with the right Waaree modules and inverter, plus a clear, all-inclusive quote.

3

Subsidy & approvals

We handle PM Surya Ghar subsidy paperwork and DISCOM approvals for you.

4

Installation

Our trained team installs your system safely and neatly, usually within a few days.

5

Net-metering

We coordinate net-meter installation so you earn credit for extra power you export.

6

Support & maintenance

Ongoing monitoring, cleaning guidance and quick service whenever you need us.
