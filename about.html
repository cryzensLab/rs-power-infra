Our story

Integrated Electrical, Energy & Infrastructure Solutions

At RSPI (RS Power and Infra), we are committed to powering progress through innovative engineering, sustainable energy solutions and quality infrastructure development. We bring together electrical expertise, modern technology and practical design to deliver reliable, efficient and future-ready solutions for commercial, industrial and residential clients.

From smart buildings and solar energy systems to industrial EPC projects, electrical control panel manufacturing and comprehensive infrastructure services, we aim to provide end-to-end solutions tailored to our clients’ needs.

Our approach is built on technical excellence, safety, sustainability and customer satisfaction. By combining engineering knowledge with innovative thinking and dependable execution, RSPI strives to create smarter spaces, stronger infrastructure and a more energy-efficient future.

In solar, we are an authorised Waaree franchise, bringing genuine products from one of India’s largest solar manufacturers, backed by our own local team for site survey, design, approvals, installation and after-sales support.

From first call to first unit generated

Free site survey
