Our story

Solar for industries, smart control for buildings

RSPI (RS Power and Infra) is an authorised Waaree solar dealer. Our main work is solar for industries: factories, warehouses and commercial sites that want lower electricity costs. We also install solar for homes and housing societies.

Our second service is building automation: controlling a building’s lights, switches, fans, AC and more from one app or panel.

Our own team handles every step, from site survey to installation and after-sales support.
