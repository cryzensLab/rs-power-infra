Solar Water Pumps

Water for your fields from the sun: no diesel, no electricity bills.

Solar water pump

Free daytime water, year after year

Solar panels run the pump directly, so you get water when the sun is up, exactly when crops need it most.

No running cost

No diesel to buy and no electricity bill for pumping.

Works without the grid

Ideal where power supply is weak, limited to night hours or missing.

Low maintenance

Few moving parts; occasional panel cleaning keeps it running for years.

Pump options

The right pump for your water source

For borewells

Submersible pumps

Installed inside the borewell to lift water from deep underground.

For open water

Surface pumps

For wells, ponds, canals and tanks where water is close to the surface.
