Residential solar

Solar for homeowners

We also install rooftop solar for homes and housing societies, and help with the PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

Government of India scheme

PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana

Get a central government subsidy of up to ₹78,000 on rooftop solar for your home and enjoy up to 300 units of free electricity every month. We guide you through registration, paperwork, installation and net-metering.

  • ₹78,000Max. subsidy for homes
  • 300Units free / month*
  • ~7%Collateral-free loans*
Check My Eligibility Official portal
